Going to your first meeting

You don't need to be sure of anything, and you don't have to say a word. Meetings are free, and nobody asks for your full name. Open meetings welcome anyone, including family and friends; closed meetings are for people who want help themselves.

Lantern is free and always will be. Private, no sign-up.
